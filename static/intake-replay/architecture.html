Three views of the intake app

Architecture, data flow, and user flow

Architecture: what runs where. Data flow: how one conversation becomes filled sections on four NHTD waiver forms. User flow: what staff and the client do, step by step.

The split: Claude reads what was meant, every fact cites the words actually said, plain code checks citations and routes each fact to every form section that uses it, and the coordinator confirms before one person files the packet. Official forms are filed as-is; signatures stay on each form.
